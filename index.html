Good To Know

Questions, Answered

Where are you located?

We are at 333 N Dobson Rd, Suite 136, in Chandler, Arizona — just off Dobson Road, with parking at the plaza.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 10 AM to 6 PM. We are closed on Tuesdays and Sundays.

How do I book?

Call us at (602) 835-9774 during open hours, send the consultation form on this page, or tap the gold button on this site to talk with our front desk and book a consultation.

What happens at a consultation?

Your consultation is free. We talk through what you want to work on, explain the treatments that fit, and answer your questions — no pressure either way.

Can I reach you after hours?

Yes. Tap the gold button on this site any time to talk with our front desk and book a consultation, even when the studio is closed. If you call and we miss you, leave a message and we will get back to you.
