Laser Lipo

Laser lipo is a non-invasive, non-surgical alternative to traditional liposuction. Instead of incisions, it relies on low-level laser energy — and instead of removing or destroying fat cells, it works by shrinking them, for a less aggressive treatment with zero downtime. You are in and out the same visit.

It can be used on the areas that store fat — abdomen, waistline, thighs and arms among them — and it is often where an Ibeauty body shaping plan begins. At your free consultation we will talk through the areas you want to work on and map out a plan before you book a single session.

Our advice to every client is simple, and it comes straight from how we work: keep your water intake up, move a little, and make light changes to how you eat. The treatments support your routine — they work best as part of it.

Cavitation

Cavitation — you will also see it called ultrasonic cavitation or lipo cavitation — uses ultrasonic waves to break down stored fat cells in the area being treated. Like everything at Ibeauty, it is non-invasive: no surgery, no downtime, in and out the same visit.

Cavitation and laser lipo are natural partners, and many Ibeauty plans pair the two in the same visit — ultrasonic waves on the stored fat, laser pads on the same area. Which combination fits you, and how many sessions make sense, is exactly what your free consultation is for.

Radio Frequency Skin Tightening

Radio frequency skin tightening pulls your collagen for a tighter feel and look. Using radio frequency technology, it is aimed at wrinkles and excess skin — the looseness that can be left behind after weight changes, or that simply comes as skin matures.

Ibeauty offers RF skin tightening for the body and for the face, and it pairs naturally with laser lipo and cavitation: contour first, then tighten. At your consultation we will look at the areas you want to address and tell you plainly where skin tightening fits.

Vacuum Therapy

Vacuum therapy is Ibeauty's non-surgical butt lift — no surgery, no downtime. Vacuum suction pulls, lifts and firms the collagen in the buttocks area, and sessions include shaping and enhancement work, so the area is treated as a whole rather than in pieces.

It is also part of how Ibeauty approaches contouring overall: in our body contouring work, vacuum suction and rollers are used alongside radio frequency to help drain through the lymphatic system. If a lift is your goal, start with a free consultation and we will map out your sessions.
